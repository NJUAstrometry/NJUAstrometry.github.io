Oleg Titov and Fengchun Shu will visit the School of Astronomy and Space Science at Nanjing University on September 6, 2024.

<!--more-->

During the visit, Dr. Titov will give a talk titled “Future Improvements of the International Celestial Reference Frame.” He will discuss how VLBI data processing techniques can improve the precision of the next-generation International Celestial Reference Frame and exchange ideas with our astrometry group.

Further details are available in the [seminar announcement](https://astronomy.nju.edu.cn/xshd/xsbg/20240905/i284319.html).
