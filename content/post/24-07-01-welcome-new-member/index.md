A warm welcome to [Ibnu Nurul Huda]({{< relref "/authors/inhuda" >}}), who joins our group as a postdoctoral researcher.

<!--more-->

Ibnu obtained his bachelor's degree from the Bandung Institute of Technology, Indonesia, and his PhD from the Paris Observatory, France, in 2019. He joined our group in June 2024 for a two-year postdoctoral appointment (2024–2026).

His research will focus on improving Earth rotation models and analyzing VLBI data.
