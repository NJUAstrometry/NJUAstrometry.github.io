abstract: >
  We investigate the use of AllWISE as an infrared celestial reference frame
  through comparison with Gaia DR3. The analysis considers approximately
  570,000 extragalactic sources and a full sample of 273 million sources,
  examining systematic differences in positions and proper motions.
