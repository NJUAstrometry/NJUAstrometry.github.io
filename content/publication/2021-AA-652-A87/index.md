abstract: >
  We compare ICRF3 S/X, K, and X/Ka positions with Gaia EDR3 for 512 common sources.
  After aligning the reference frames, we examine radio–optical offsets and their
  relationships with observing frequency and source properties. The offsets correlate
  statistically with radio structure. The S/X frame remains the preferred choice
  for alignment with the Gaia celestial reference frame.
