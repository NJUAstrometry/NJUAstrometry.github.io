abstract: >
  We evaluate the stability of the ICRF3 axes using source position time series
  derived from S/X-band VLBI observations between August 1979 and December 2020.
  We assess both the global spin and the scatter of yearly frame orientations.
  Each spin component is no larger than 0.8 μas/yr, with an uncertainty of
  0.3 μas/yr. Yearly axis orientations have standard deviations of 10–20 μas,
  with no degradation in stability after the adoption of ICRF3.
